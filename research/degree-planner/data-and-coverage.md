# What Flow can claim

[Study overview](README.md) · [Planner experience](experience.md) · [Release and validation](testing-and-release.md)

## Where best effort helps, and where it hurts

A rough suggestion is useful if the student can see why it was suggested and what remains uncertain. A rough statement that a graduation requirement is satisfied can change whether someone registers for their final needed course. My recommendation is **partial coverage, precise claims**. Flow can be useful for every faculty while acknowledging gaps in a particular program, year, personal record, or future offering.

| Question | Evidence that supports an answer | What the student sees when it is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does this meet a requirement? | A reviewed rule for the selected calendar and credential combination, sufficient evidence from the student’s record, and a valid allocation. | “We need your grade for this check” or “Residency rules for this program have not been reviewed.” Keep these apart from a genuinely outstanding requirement. |
| Would this plan cover it? | Named planned courses, applicable conditions, and enough information to evaluate the proposed plan. | “Reserved for an upper-level elective. Choose a course to check this requirement.” Flag a plan that covers enough units on paper but has an unresolved prerequisite. |
| Can I take it in this term? | Requisites, restrictions and, where available, a current published offering. Seat availability and actual registration are separate facts. | “Usually offered in fall, next fall unconfirmed” or “Departmental consent needed.” Show the date and source of the evidence. [Waterloo Schedule of Classes](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/academics/academics-undergraduate-students/registering-courses/understanding-schedule-classes) · [Course selection and enrolment](https://uwaterloo.ca/science-undergraduate-office/academic-information/course-enrolment-resources) |
| What would I enjoy or manage? | The student’s stated interests, term constraints and student experiences with the course. | Show course descriptions and alternatives. Keep candidates with little community data visible. |
| Am I ready to graduate? | A whole-degree assessment also depends on the university’s record, milestones, individual rulings, and official processes. | “All 8 checked groups are covered in your plan. Two items need review.” Name them and prepare an advisor question. With complete supported coverage, say exactly which conditions are met on the entered record and which still depend on future outcomes. [Waterloo SE graduation process](https://uwaterloo.ca/software-engineering/undergraduate-students/graduation-information) |

**Why I would avoid one progress ring.** Suppose a student has earned 19.75 of 20 units, still needs a specific course, and has one pending transfer ruling. “98.75%” answers a units question only. The more useful summary is “0.25 units short. One named requirement open. One decision pending.” Beside that, offer “See how next term changes this.” A percentage can work inside a homogeneous group if its denominator is explicit.

## University, student, and community contributions

| Input | What I would use it for | Product boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undergraduate Calendar, archived editions and addenda | Program combinations, formal requirements, course requisites, units and rules on sharing credit. Keep links on the affected group and capture the edition used. | Store the applicable edition per credential when the policy calls for it. Surface corrections and their effect on the student’s plan. [How to read the calendar](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/academics/academics-undergraduate-students/how-navigate-academic-calendar) · [Archived editions](https://uwaterloo.ca/academic-calendar/archives) · [CS guidance on different calendars](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/checklists) |
| Faculty sequences, checklists, and advising pages | Generate a starter outline and plain-language explanations. Preserve the difference between a policy, a recommended order, and a sample elective. | If the checklist and calendar disagree, flag the affected claim and refer to the governing source. Use departmental advice to explain common choices. [CS checklist guidance](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/checklists) · [Public Health program requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/public-health-sciences/current-undergraduate-students/majors-minors-specializations/program-requirements) |
| Published schedules and Waterloo Open Data | Show confirmed or tentative term-level offering information as available. Historical offerings may inform a clearly labelled estimate. | Seat availability is volatile. A plausible future term is insufficient for an unconditional “last chance” or “you will graduate late” message. [Waterloo Open Data API documentation](https://openapi.data.uwaterloo.ca/api-docs/index.html) · [Schedule of Classes](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/academics/academics-undergraduate-students/registering-courses/understanding-schedule-classes) |
| Student-entered facts and records | Prior attempts, external units, course outcomes, grades where needed, intended workload, and personal decisions. | Let the student preview a paste, correct it, delete it, and export the plan. Prefer local parsing and disposal of raw transcript text after review. A reported approval stays labelled with its origin. |
| Flow reviews and useful/easy/liked ratings | Help compare eligible courses and expose qualitative details a calendar cannot answer. Show count and recency when known. Let the student choose the sorting criterion. | Ease is a self-reported judgement. Never convert it into predicted personal grades, requirements, or promised workload hours. Carry forward missing-data candidates. [Flow course ratings](https://uwflow.com/explore) · [Student discussion of differing offerings](https://www.reddit.com/r/uwaterloo/comments/1tnqx7f/ne_476ece_634/) |
| Community examples or corrections | Later, invite an annotated elective shortlist or a report against a specific rule. A maintainer can verify the attached calendar evidence. | Keep personal exceptions personal. Publish a sample pathway only with consent, cohort, scope, and date. Avoid public transcripts, copying another person’s approvals, or popularity as the default route. |

I would defer “students like you usually take…” until Flow knows it has consent, enough observations for the actual cohort, and a way to avoid exposing small groups. Published sequences, optional community reviews, and a student's own interests can deliver the first release. Public Reddit threads help discover product needs. Importing Reddit opinions into student plans would be a separate product decision with little initial payoff.

## Irregular records are ordinary planning cases

| Situation | How the student represents it | What Flow changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failed, withdrawn, repeated, or incomplete course | Keep each attempt and its actual outcome. A student can pencil in a retry or record a later resolution. | Re-evaluate eligible credit, grade thresholds, averages and downstream prerequisites using the applicable rules. Never assume every repeat replaces the original grade. Engineering gives an example where a successful supplemental exam clears a failure while the original grade remains in averages. [Engineering course and exam guidance](https://uwaterloo.ca/engineering/undergraduate-students/academic-support/course-and-exam-information) |
| Prerequisite waiver, seat permission, or load permission | Record exactly what was authorized, for which course or section and term, conditions, source, and whether it is requested or granted. | A prerequisite waiver may permit entry to a course. It awards no units for the missing prerequisite. A seat permission resolves only its named restriction. These distinctions appear on the university form. [Waterloo Course Override Form](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/course_override.pdf) |
| Substitution or waived degree requirement | Attach the decision to one requirement in one credential. Keep the wording the student received. | Show the ordinary rule result and the result using the reported decision. If a requirement was waived, evaluate total degree units independently. Some substitutions can depend on a specific topic or decision maker. [Public Health sequence and discretionary substitutions](https://uwaterloo.ca/health/current-undergraduates/course-sequences/2025-2026-honours-public-health-regular-course-sequence) |
| Laurier cross-registration | Add the host course and term, the known Waterloo equivalent if listed, or a pending recognition question. For joint degrees, keep the home institution of each credential and show available rule coverage separately. | Tie equivalent identifiers to the same earned credit. Distinguish permission to enrol from the use of a course toward a specific requirement. The Waterloo process differs when an equivalent appears on the Quest schedule. [Waterloo’s Laurier cross-registration guidance](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/academics/academics-undergraduate-students/registering-courses/dropadd-period) |
| Exchange, transfer, or advanced standing | Keep the original source, host course and units, and proposed Waterloo recognition. Allow one host course to produce a specific equivalent, generic units, a mixture, or no recognized credit. | A pending match can be explored conditionally. A generic subject credit can serve a unit or breadth rule when eligible. A named prerequisite needs corresponding evidence. Record final awarded units once known, counting the underlying credit only once. [Arts exchange and credit examples](https://uwaterloo.ca/arts/undergraduate/international-exchange) |
| Mixed work/study, reduced load, leave, or placement | Name the term activity and available capacity, including zero. Optionally protect the return term or an employer placement. The student can set a lower personal limit without explaining why. | Re-evaluate prerequisites and timing and flag any published policy requiring permission for the change. A model that automatically inserts a “spare” course in a work term ignores the student’s constraint. [Engineering course load guidance](https://uwaterloo.ca/engineering/undergraduate-students/academic-support/altering-course-load) · [Waterloo work-term course guidance](https://uwaterloo.ca/the-centre/academics/academics-undergraduate-students/registering-courses/dropadd-period) |
| Milestone or non-course condition | Add a co-op, practicum, average, residency, or other obligation with its own evidence and status. | Show pass/fail/unknown only where the relevant rule and evidence support it. If the source requires human judgement, carry the item to the advisor summary. |

Manual notes are the escape hatch for an unfamiliar situation. A note can be attached to a term or requirement and displayed in the advisor summary. It changes an evaluated result only when its effect is explicitly represented. A student who does not want warnings about a known issue may dismiss a reminder while keeping the underlying condition visible.

## Keeping it credible after the original team leaves

1. **Publish coverage by credential, edition, and requirement family.** Report named core courses, breadth, averages, residency, milestones and combinations separately as applicable. Give each unsupported area a plain-language description. Students should know coverage before spending time importing their record.
2. **Assign ownership to the data people will inherit.** A Flow maintainer owns release and correction handling. A faculty liaison can help review interpretations where available. Do not make routine operation depend on advisor sign-off for every student plan. New sources and corrections need a recorded reviewer, edition, and test case.
3. **Separate annual changes from corrections.** Keep a saved plan on its chosen calendar. A new edition creates an optional comparison. A correction to the *same* edition should prompt re-evaluation, identify affected students’ plan items in-product, and preserve their choices. Allow rollback of a bad rule without undoing students’ edits.
4. **Maintain worked cases across cohorts.** Include a legal overlap, a forbidden overlap, a quarter-unit credit, an old course code, an exchange equivalence, a grade threshold, and an unmodelled milestone. Compare outcomes and the displayed explanations before publishing. A course that moved from one box to another may materially change the answer.
5. **Give disputes a narrow route to resolution.** “Report a problem with this requirement” carries the exact rule version and an optional redacted example, under the student’s control. Classify an error as a record mismatch, a rule problem, or an individual exception. Only the second should change results for other students.

The enduring expense is reviewing what changes and supporting the claims attached to it. If maintenance capacity shrinks, narrow the documented coverage and keep the manual planner usable. An old rule set should retain its edition and review date. Students should see which new cohorts have yet to be reviewed.
